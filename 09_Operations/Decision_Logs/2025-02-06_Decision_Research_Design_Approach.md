## Decision Required

Adopt quasi-experimental pre/post design as the primary research design for the praxis (pending full methodology framework approval).

## Context

- Agent 02 (Applied Research Methodologist) produced first-run validity notes for a quasi-experimental design (internal validity threats and mitigations).
- Engineering doctorate praxis typically requires an applied artifact with measurable outcomes; randomized controlled trials are often infeasible in operational cybersecurity settings.
- Proposal and full methodology chapter need a single, defensible design choice.

## Options

1. **Quasi-experimental pre/post (single group)**  
   - Pros: Feasible, clear before/after comparison, aligns with many artifact evaluations.  
   - Cons: Internal validity threats (history, maturation, instrumentation) require explicit mitigations and documentation.

2. **Quasi-experimental with matched comparison group**  
   - Pros: Stronger internal validity if a suitable comparison exists.  
   - Cons: Requires access to a comparable group and more data; may extend timeline.

3. **Case study / design science**  
   - Pros: Fits artifact development and rich description.  
   - Cons: Different validity framework; may need advisor alignment on “generalizability” language.

## Recommendation

Option 1 (quasi-experimental pre/post, single group) as the **default**, with mitigations from `02_Agents/02_Applied_Research_Methodologist/Outputs/First_Run_Validity_Notes.md` carried into `03_Research_Methods/Validity_Frameworks/Threats_and_Mitigations.md` once PI approves. Revisit Option 2 if a natural comparison group becomes available.

- Must be reflected in proposal and methodology chapter (Agent 09).
