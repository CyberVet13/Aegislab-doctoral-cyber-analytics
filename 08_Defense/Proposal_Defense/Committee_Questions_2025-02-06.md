## Methodology (SEAS 8499)

1. Why did you choose a quasi-experimental pre/post design rather than an experiment with random assignment?  
   *Difficulty: Medium*

2. How have you addressed internal validity threats, especially history and instrumentation?  
   *Difficulty: Medium*

3. What is your planned sample size and power, and what if you cannot achieve it?  
   *Difficulty: Medium*

4. How do you define detection rate operationally, and how will you ensure the same definition in pre and post?  
   *Difficulty: Easy*

5. What are the main limitations of your design for causal inference and generalizability?
