## Likely questions and response prompts

1. **Why quasi-experimental rather than experimental?**  
   Operational cybersecurity context; RCT infeasible (access, ethics, unit of analysis). Pre/post with documented internal validity mitigations is standard in applied security research.

2. **How did you address internal validity?**  
   Explicit threat table (history, maturation, testing, instrumentation, selection) with mitigations in Validity_Frameworks; fixed measurement window, stable instrumentation, documented timeline and external events.

3. **What are the main limitations?**  
   Causal inference is tentative; generalizability limited to similar contexts; single group. Stated in report and limitations section.

4. **How did you choose your metrics?**  
   Aligned with research questions and SEAS 8410/8414; operational definitions in methodology; baseline and post same measures.

5. **Power and sample size?**  
   Target power 0.80; effect size and n documented in `03_Research_Methods/Research_Design/Analysis_Plan.md`. If achievable n is below powered n, state as limitation and report CIs; avoid overclaiming null results.
