# Ethical Boundaries Statement — AegisLab Doctoral Research

**Purpose:** Explicit scope and prohibitions for committee and institutional alignment.  
**Alignment:** 00_Governance Academic_Integrity_Policy, AI_Use_Disclosure; SEAS 8499/8188.

---

## 1. In scope (permitted)

- Defensive threat modeling and mitigation design (e.g., ATT&CK/D3FEND mapping; no exploit development).
- Design and evaluation of detection/analytics artifact with authorized or synthetic data.
- Use of AI tools as research assistants with full disclosure and PI review (00_Governance).
- Data analysis and visualization for research outcomes with documented provenance and no PII exposure without approval.
- Publication and defense of research with honest limitations and AI disclosure.

---

## 2. Out of scope (prohibited)

- **No offensive or unauthorized activity:** No penetration testing, exploitation, or unauthorized access to systems or data.
- **No malware or attack tooling:** No development, deployment, or use of malware or offensive tools; threat model is defensive understanding only.
- **No PII/sensitive data misuse:** No use of PII or sensitive data in pipeline or analysis without IRB and data governance approval; no re-identification or exposure.
- **No misrepresentation:** No fabrication of data, results, or citations; no omission of AI assistance where required.
- **No scope creep:** Research and artifact stay within approved scope and institutional/legal boundaries.

---

## 3. Cybersecurity research ethics

- Authorized data and systems only; responsible disclosure if vulnerabilities are discovered in authorized scope.
- Coordination with organizational security if research touches operational assets; document in Ethics_Approvals or methodology.

---

## 4. Committee communication

- This statement can be cited in proposal and final report; include in defense materials if required by institution.
- PI certifies adherence; update if scope or institutional requirements change (Change_Log).
