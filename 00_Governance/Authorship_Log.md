Track the authorship and contribution sources for all research artifacts, ensuring clear attribution of human vs. AI contributions.

## Contribution Percentage Guidelines

- **0-25% AI**: Minor assistance (grammar, formatting, citation formatting)
- **26-50% AI**: Moderate assistance (literature summaries, code scaffolds, initial drafts)
- **51-75% AI**: Substantial assistance (technical writing, complex analysis scripts, architecture diagrams)
- **76-100% AI**: Extensive assistance (requires significant PI revision and validation)

**Note:** Percentages are estimates. Higher AI contribution requires more rigorous PI validation and documentation.

## Validation Methods

- **Independent Verification**: Cross-check against primary sources
- **Peer Review**: External expert review of technical accuracy
- **Reproducibility Test**: Re-run analysis with documented procedures
- **Committee Feedback**: Incorporate advisor/committee input
- **Multi-Model Cross-Check**: Validate using different AI models
