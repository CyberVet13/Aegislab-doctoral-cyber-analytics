# Academic Integrity Policy - AegisLab Doctoral Research Environment

## Purpose
This policy establishes standards for maintaining academic integrity throughout the Doctor of Engineering in Cybersecurity Analytics program while utilizing AI-assisted research tools within the AegisLab environment.

## Core Principles

### 1. Human Authority
- The Principal Investigator (PI) retains final decision authority on all research outputs
- All AI-generated content must be reviewed, validated, and approved by the PI before incorporation
- The PI is responsible for the intellectual contribution and originality of the research

### 2. Transparency Requirements
- All AI tool usage must be documented in `00_Governance/AI_Use_Disclosure.md`
- Each AI-assisted output must include metadata: date, model used, prompt summary, and PI review status
- Session logs must be maintained in `09_Operations/Session_Logs/` with timestamps and model identifiers

### 3. Original Contribution Standards
- AI tools serve as research assistants, not co-authors
- The PI must demonstrate original intellectual synthesis, critical analysis, and novel contributions
- Literature reviews must include independent source validation beyond AI-provided references
- Research design decisions must be justified with PI reasoning documented

### 4. Citation and Attribution
- AI-generated summaries of existing work must trace back to primary sources
- All claims must be supported by peer-reviewed literature or original empirical evidence
- When AI assists in literature discovery, the PI must independently verify and read cited sources
- Paraphrasing or synthesis assisted by AI must be disclosed and independently validated

### 5. Data Integrity
- All datasets must have documented provenance in `05_Data/README.md`
- AI-generated synthetic data must be clearly labeled and methodology disclosed
- Analysis code must be version-controlled with clear authorship attribution
- Results must be reproducible with documented procedures

### 6. Committee Defensibility
- All research artifacts must be explainable without reliance on "the AI said so"
- The PI must be able to defend methodological choices, analytical decisions, and conclusions independently
- Mock defense sessions (Agent 10) identify areas requiring deeper PI understanding

## Prohibited Practices

- Submitting AI-generated text as original work without substantial PI revision and validation
- Using AI to fabricate data, results, or citations
- Relying on AI for methodological decisions without independent justification
- Failing to disclose AI assistance in any research output
- Bypassing human review for any committee-facing deliverable

## Enforcement and Monitoring

- Monthly self-audits using `00_Governance/Authorship_Log.md`
- Pre-submission checklist requiring AI disclosure review
- Advisor consultation on borderline cases
- Committee notification of AI tool usage per institutional requirements
